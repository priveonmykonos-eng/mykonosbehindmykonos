It's a revelation. A journey through history, myth, and the essence of an island that has captivated travelers for centuries, unveiling the hidden pulse beneath its dazzling surface. A dive into the magic of the island.

First stop

Mykonos Town, on foot

Stop 01

Mykonos Town

Mykonos, a place where time seems to pause yet beats to an irresistible rhythm, invites you to go beyond the surface, deep into its soul. Your guide leads you on foot through the whitewashed alleys of the Chora, past the harbor and the churches, into corners no vehicle could ever reach.

Stop 02

The Windmills

Towering against the sky, the iconic windmills stand as eternal guardians, overlooking the Aegean. From here, Mykonos unfolds like a painting, white and blue, where the horizon fades into infinity and the sea calls to the traveler.

By private vehicle

Beyond the town

Stop 03

Ftelia

A wide, open bay where the wind off the Aegean meets untouched sand. Fewer crowds, more horizon, a quiet pause before the road climbs back into the hills.

Stop 04

Ano Mera & Panagia Tourliani

Beyond the well-trodden paths, where Mykonos reveals its true self, lies Ano Mera, a village untouched by time. In the Panagia Tourliani Monastery, candlelight flickers over sacred icons, the bells chime softly, and a deep sense of peace lingers in the air. In the village square, a traditional taverna welcomes you with the aroma of grilled souvlaki and local wine, offering an authentic taste of Greek hospitality, and your own free meze and ouzo.

Stop 05

Armenistis Lighthouse

For those seeking more, the Armenistis Lighthouse stands at the edge of the cliffs, watching over the endless blue for more than a century. The waves crash below, the wind hums through the rocks, and the Aegean stretches infinitely, whispering tales of sailors past.

Stop 06

Kalafatis & Elia Beach

An extraordinary experience. A discovery of a place that is more than an image, it is a feeling. Two of the island's most beautiful beaches, and many more places to discover before the day is through.
